How the numbers are built

  • Source. Year-end 2025 Asset and Liability Statements published by the Bermuda Monetary Authority, covering — reported positions. This page shows summaries only. The position-level data stays in the local database.
  • Invested assets are the base for every percentage: bonds and loans, structured assets, mortgages and investment items in Other Assets. Separate accounts, derivatives, reinsurance assets, deferred acquisition costs, tax and receivables are excluded.
  • Currency. Amounts are in US dollars at the reporting-date rate. Market value is used as filed, with book value or loan balance only where market value is blank.
  • Units. Some filers report in thousands or millions without always saying so. Where the filing is unclear the multiplier was inferred, and the company carries a data note.
  • Groups add legal entities together. Business reinsured between entities is not removed, so group totals can double count.
  • Credit. Below investment grade means BSCR classes 5 to 8, where 0 is government and 8 the lowest.
  • Accounting. Filers use US GAAP or IFRS. Reserves are not adjusted between the two, so reserve ratios compare less cleanly across them.
  • Backing was researched from public sources in November 2026 and is not in the filings. Many tags rest on the company name alone, as the source column says. Check a tag before relying on it.
  • Hybrid means an insurer and an alternative manager or outside investors back the company together.
